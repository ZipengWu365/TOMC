# Community conduct

Treat contributors with respect. Critique claims and code with evidence; welcome questions and corrections. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, disclosure of private information, and repeated disruptive behavior are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content or restrict participation. Reports can be sent through GitHub's private reporting channels where available. Do not include another person's private information in a public issue. Maintainers should handle reports consistently and allow a good-faith explanation or appeal.
